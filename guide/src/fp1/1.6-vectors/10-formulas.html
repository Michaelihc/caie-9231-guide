Formulas line $\V r=\V a+\lambda\V b$, plane $\V r\cdot\V n=p$

F1
Scalar productMF19 $$\V a\cdot\V b=a_1b_1+a_2b_2+a_3b_3=|\V a||\V b|\cos\theta$$

$\V a\cdot\V b=0$ ⇔ ⊥.

F2
Vector product
$$\V a\times\V b=\cvec{a_2b_3-a_3b_2}{a_3b_1-a_1b_3}{a_1b_2-a_2b_1},\qquad |\V a\times\V b|=|\V a||\V b|\sin\theta$$

Answer ⊥ both. $\V b\times\V a=-\V a\times\V b$. $=\V 0$ ⇔ ∥. Index pattern: 23−32, 31−13, 12−21.

F3
Line, cartesian
$$\V r=\V a+\lambda\V b\iff\frac{x-a_1}{b_1}=\frac{y-a_2}{b_2}=\frac{z-a_3}{b_3}\;(=\lambda)$$

Some $b_i=0$ ⇒ drop that fraction, write that coord fixed, e.g. $z=a_3$.

F4
Plane, 3 forms
$$n_1x+n_2y+n_3z=p\iff\V r\cdot\V n=p,\quad p=\V a\cdot\V n\qquad\V r=\V a+\lambda\V b+\mu\V c\;\Rightarrow\;\V n=\V b\times\V c$$

$\V a$ = any point in plane. $\V n$ = normal.

F5
Distance point $(x_0,y_0,z_0)$ to plane
$$d=\frac{|n_1x_0+n_2y_0+n_3z_0-p|}{\sqrt{n_1^2+n_2^2+n_3^2}}$$

From O: $|p|/|\V n|$. Same sign inside $|\,|$ for two points ⇒ same side.

F6
Distance point $Q$ to line
$$d=\frac{|(\V q-\V a)\times\V b|}{|\V b|}$$
F7
Shortest distance, skew lines
$$\V r=\V a+\lambda\V b,\ \V r=\V c+\mu\V d:\qquad d=\frac{|(\V c-\V a)\cdot(\V b\times\V d)|}{|\V b\times\V d|}$$

$d=0$ ⇒ lines meet.

F8
Angles (acute)
$$\text{line,line: }\cos\theta=\frac{|\V b\cdot\V d|}{|\V b||\V d|}\quad\text{plane,plane: }\cos\theta=\frac{|\V n_1\cdot\V n_2|}{|\V n_1||\V n_2|}\quad\text{line,plane: }\sin\theta=\frac{|\V b\cdot\V n|}{|\V b||\V n|}$$

Line, plane uses sin: $\V n$ is ⊥ plane. Abs value ⇒ acute.

F9
Area, volume
$$\triangle ABC=\tfrac12|\AB{AB}\times\AB{AC}|\qquad V_{ABCD}=\tfrac16\left|\AB{AB}\cdot(\AB{AC}\times\AB{AD})\right|$$

Parallelogram: $|\AB{AB}\times\AB{AC}|$. Tetrahedron also $\tfrac13\times\text{base}\times\text{height}$ (Q usually gives this).

F10
Point dividing $AB$ in ratio $\lambda:\mu$
$$\V p=\frac{\mu\V a+\lambda\V b}{\lambda+\mu}$$

Was in old MF10. Gone from MF19.
