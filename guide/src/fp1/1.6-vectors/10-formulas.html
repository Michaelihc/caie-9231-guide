F1
Scalar productMF19 $$\V a\cdot\V b=a_1b_1+a_2b_2+a_3b_3=|\V a||\V b|\cos\theta$$

$\V a\cdot\V b=0$ ⇔ ⊥.

Dot = length of arrow 1 × shadow of arrow 2 on it. Shadow 0 at 90°, negative past 90°.

F2
Vector product
$$\V a\times\V b=\cvec{a_2b_3-a_3b_2}{a_3b_1-a_1b_3}{a_1b_2-a_2b_1},\qquad |\V a\times\V b|=|\V a||\V b|\sin\theta$$

Answer ⊥ both. $\V b\times\V a=-\V a\times\V b$. $=\V 0$ ⇔ ∥.

Top = 2nd·3rd − 3rd·2nd. Next rows: shift indices 1 → 23, 31, 12.

Size = size of $\V a$ × size of $\V b$ × sin(angle) = area of parallelogram on them. Points ⊥ both.

No quick derivation. Learn it. Memory aid: $\begin{vmatrix}\V i&\V j&\V k\\a_1&a_2&a_3\\b_1&b_2&b_3\end{vmatrix}$, middle term minus.

F3
Plane, 3 forms
$$n_1x+n_2y+n_3z=p\iff\V r\cdot\V n=p,\quad p=\V a\cdot\V n\qquad\V r=\V a+\lambda\V b+\mu\V c\;\Rightarrow\;\V n=\V b\times\V c$$

$\V a$ = any point in plane. $\V n$ = normal. $\V r\cdot\V n\ne0$: $\V r$ starts at O, not in plane. $p=0$ ⇔ plane through O.

Normal · (any point in plane) = same number every time.

Normal = cross of 2 plane directions.

  1. Pick a known point in plane.
  2. (any point in plane − known point) lies flat in plane ⇒ ⊥ normal ⇒ dot with normal $=0$.
  3. Expand: (any point)·normal − (known point)·normal $=0$ ⇒ (any point)·normal = (known point)·normal. That number is $p$.
  4. Expand dot with $(x,y,z)$ ⇒ cartesian.
  5. Normal ⊥ both plane directions ⇒ normal = plane direction 1 × plane direction 2 (F2).
F4
Distance point $(x_0,y_0,z_0)$ to plane
$$d=\frac{|n_1x_0+n_2y_0+n_3z_0-p|}{\sqrt{n_1^2+n_2^2+n_3^2}}$$

From O: $|p|/|\V n|$. Same sign inside $|\,|$ for two points ⇒ same side.

Plug point into plane's left side. Subtract right side. Make positive. ÷ length of normal.

Picture: point's shadow − plane's shadow, both on normal line.

  1. Find normal of plane.
  2. Set up: foot + $k\times$normal = point.
  3. Foot is on plane ⇒ foot = point − $k\times$normal, sub into plane equation.
  4. Solve for $k$.
  5. Distance = $|k|\times|$normal$|$.

Foot and reflection: S4.

F5
Distance point $Q$ to line
$$d=\frac{|(\V q-\V a)\times\V b|}{|\V b|}$$

Cross (line point → point) with line direction. Take length. ÷ length of line direction.

Picture: parallelogram area ÷ base = height.

  1. Foot is on line ⇒ foot = line point + $t\times$line direction.
  2. (point − foot) ⊥ line direction ⇒ dot $=0$.
  3. Solve for $t$ ⇒ foot.
  4. Distance = |point − foot|.

Why the formula works: parallelogram on (point − line point) and line direction. Area = |cross| = base × height.

F6
Shortest distance, skew lines
$$\V r=\V a+\lambda\V b,\ \V r=\V c+\mu\V d:\qquad d=\frac{|(\V c-\V a)\cdot(\V b\times\V d)|}{|\V b\times\V d|}$$

$d=0$ ⇒ lines meet.

Join any point on $l_1$ to any point on $l_2$. Dot with common normal ($\V b\times\V d$). Make positive. ÷ length of normal.

Picture: shadow of any join on common normal. Same however you slide.

  1. Normal = line 1 direction × line 2 direction (⊥ both lines).
  2. Set up: point on line 1 + $k\times$normal = point on line 2.
  3. Each point has its own parameter ⇒ 3 equations, 3 unknowns.
  4. Solve. Distance = $|k|\times|$normal$|$.

Bonus: the two parameters give both ends ⇒ common ⊥ (S9).

F7
Angles (acute)
$$\text{line,line: }\cos\theta=\frac{|\V b\cdot\V d|}{|\V b||\V d|}\quad\text{plane,plane: }\cos\theta=\frac{|\V n_1\cdot\V n_2|}{|\V n_1||\V n_2|}\quad\text{line,plane: }\sin\theta=\frac{|\V b\cdot\V n|}{|\V b||\V n|}$$

Line, plane uses sin: $\V n$ is ⊥ plane. Abs value ⇒ acute.

cos(angle) = |dot| ÷ (length × length).

Planes: use normals. Line with plane: use sin, because normal sits 90° off plane.

  1. Start from F1 (in MF19): cos(angle) = dot ÷ (length × length).
  2. Two lines ⇒ use line directions. Two planes ⇒ use the normals.
  3. Line and plane ⇒ F1 with line direction and plane normal gives angle to normal. Plane is 90° off normal ⇒ answer = 90° − that.
  4. Take abs value of dot ⇒ acute.
F8
Area, volume
$$\triangle ABC=\tfrac12|\AB{AB}\times\AB{AC}|\qquad V_{ABCD}=\tfrac16\left|\AB{AB}\cdot(\AB{AC}\times\AB{AD})\right|$$

Parallelogram: $|\AB{AB}\times\AB{AC}|$. Tetrahedron also $\tfrac13\times\text{base}\times\text{height}$ (Q usually gives this).

Triangle = half length of cross of 2 sides from one corner.

Tetrahedron = ⅙ × |side · (side × side)|, 3 sides from one corner.

Picture: 3rd edge's shadow on cross = height. Cross length = base area.

  1. Triangle: ½ × side × side × sin(angle). |side × side| = side × side × sin(angle) ⇒ ½|side × side|.
  2. Tetrahedron: ⅓ × base area × height.
  3. Base area: triangle rule on 2 edges from one corner.
  4. Height: distance from 4th corner to base plane (F4 method).
F9
Point dividing $AB$ in ratio $\lambda:\mu$
$$\V p=\frac{\mu\V a+\lambda\V b}{\lambda+\mu}$$

Was in old MF10. Gone from MF19.

Start at $A$. Go $\frac{\lambda}{\lambda+\mu}$ of the way to $B$.

  1. Start at $A$. Add fraction $\frac{\lambda}{\lambda+\mu}$ of $\AB{AB}$.
  2. Collect terms ⇒ $\frac{\mu\V a+\lambda\V b}{\lambda+\mu}$.

Check: $\lambda=\mu$ ⇒ midpoint.
