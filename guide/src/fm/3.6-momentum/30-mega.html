M1
Two spheres and a wall, in a lineuses S1 S2 F5

Smooth spheres $A$ (mass $m$) and $B$ (mass $2m$), equal radii, on a smooth horizontal surface. $A$ moves with speed $u$ and hits $B$, at rest, directly. Coefficient of restitution $e$.

  1. Find the velocities of $A$ and $B$ after the collision in terms of $u$ and $e$.
  2. Show that $A$'s direction of motion is reversed if $e\gt \frac12$.

Now $u=10\ \text{m s}^{-1}$, $e=\frac45$. $B$ then hits a fixed smooth wall ⊥ its path, coefficient of restitution $\frac34$.

  1. Show that $B$ hits $A$ again. Find both velocities after this collision.
  2. Show that there are no further collisions.
  3. Find the total loss of kinetic energy.
  1. start: masses and velocities$$m_A=m,\ \ m_B=2m,\qquad u_A=u,\ \ u_B=0$$
  2. (a) momentum, + towards $B$$$mu+2m(0)=mv_A+2mv_B$$
  3. NEL: separation = $e$ × approach$$v_B-v_A=e\,(u-0)$$
  4. solve the pair$$v_A=\frac{u(1-2e)}{3},\qquad v_B=\frac{u(1+e)}{3}$$
  5. (b) reversed ⇔ $v_A\lt 0$$$1-2e\lt 0\iff e\gt \tfrac12$$
  6. sub $u=10$, $e=\frac45$$$v_A=\frac{10\left(1-\frac85\right)}{3}=-2,\qquad v_B=\frac{10\left(1+\frac45\right)}{3}=6$$
  7. (c) $B$ hits wall square on: rebound = $\frac34$ × approach$$\tfrac34\times6=4.5\ \text{away from wall}$$
  8. both move away from wall; $B$ follows $A$, faster$$4.5\gt 2\ \Rightarrow\ B\text{ catches }A$$
  9. momentum, keep + towards wall$$m(-2)+2m(-4.5)=mv_A+2mv_B$$
  10. NEL: + still towards wall, so $A$ is on the − side$$v_B-v_A=\tfrac45\big({-2}-(-4.5)\big)=2$$
  11. solve the pair$$v_A=-5,\qquad v_B=-3$$
  12. (d) both away from wall, $A$ leading and faster$$5\gt 3\ \Rightarrow\ \text{no more collisions}$$
  13. (e) KE before − KE after$$\tfrac12m(10^2)-\Big[\tfrac12m(5^2)+\tfrac12(2m)(3^2)\Big]=28.5m\ \text{J}$$
M2
Oblique impact, then two wallsuses S3 S4 F5

Smooth spheres $A$ (mass $m$) and $B$ (mass $2m$), equal radii, collide on a smooth horizontal table. Just before impact each has speed $5\ \text{m s}^{-1}$; $A$'s velocity makes angle $\alpha$ with the line of centres, $B$'s makes angle $\beta$, both on the same side of it, where $\cos\alpha=\frac45$, $\cos\beta=\frac35$. Coefficient of restitution $\frac12$.

  1. Find the speed and direction of motion of each sphere after the impact.
  2. Find the angle through which $A$ is deflected.
  3. Find the loss of kinetic energy.

$A$ then hits a smooth fixed wall ⊥ the line of centres, then a second smooth fixed wall ⊥ the first. Coefficient of restitution $\frac13$ for each wall.

  1. Find $A$'s speed after the first wall and the angle its path makes with that wall.
  2. Show that after both walls $A$'s path is parallel to its path before them, and find the fraction of its KE lost in the two wall impacts.
  1. start$$u_A=u_B=5,\qquad\cos\alpha=\tfrac45,\ \ \cos\beta=\tfrac35,\qquad e=\tfrac12$$
  2. at impact: line of centres through both centres; angles from it$$\sin\alpha=\tfrac35,\qquad\sin\beta=\tfrac45$$
  3. resolve each velocity: along line of centres, across it$$A:\ \big(5\cos\alpha,\ 5\sin\alpha\big)=(4,\ 3)\qquad B:\ \big({-5}\cos\beta,\ 5\sin\beta\big)=(-3,\ 4)$$
  4. smooth ⇒ impulse only along line of centres ⇒ across parts stay$$\text{across: }A\text{ keeps }3,\quad B\text{ keeps }4$$
  5. momentum along line of centres, + from $A$ to $B$, masses in$$m(4)+2m(-3)=mx+2my$$
  6. NEL along line of centres, same signs as momentum$$y-x=\tfrac12\big(4-(-3)\big)$$
  7. solve the pair$$x=-3,\qquad y=\tfrac12$$
  8. (a) recombine each sphere: along part + its own across part$$v_A=\sqrt{3^2+3^2}=3\sqrt2,\qquad v_B=\sqrt{\left(\tfrac12\right)^2+4^2}=\tfrac{\sqrt{65}}{2}$$
  9. directions: angle with line of centres = tan⁻¹(across ÷ along)$$A:\ \tan^{-1}\tfrac33=45^\circ\text{ (moving back)},\qquad B:\ \tan^{-1}\tfrac{4}{1/2}=82.9^\circ$$
  10. (b) angle between old and new direction of $A$$$180^\circ-45^\circ-\cos^{-1}\tfrac45=135^\circ-36.9^\circ=98.1^\circ$$
  11. (c) KE before − KE after, masses in$$\tfrac12m(5^2)+\tfrac12(2m)(5^2)-\Big[\tfrac12m(3\sqrt2)^2+\tfrac12(2m)\tfrac{65}{4}\Big]=12.25m\ \text{J}$$
  12. (d) wall ⊥ line of centres: resolve again, into wall and along wall$$\text{into wall }3,\qquad\text{along wall }3$$
  13. along wall kept; into wall × $\frac13$, reversed$$\text{out }\tfrac13\times3=1\ \Rightarrow\ v=\sqrt{1^2+3^2}=\sqrt{10},\quad\tan^{-1}\tfrac13=18.4^\circ\text{ to wall}$$
  14. (e) wall 2 ⊥ wall 1: along wall 1 is into wall 2$$\text{into wall 2 }3,\qquad\text{along wall 2 }1$$
  15. along kept; into × $\frac13$, reversed$$\text{out }\tfrac13\times3=1,\quad\text{along }1$$
  16. compare with the velocity before wall 1$$(1,\ {-1})=-\tfrac13\,({-3},\ 3)\ \Rightarrow\ \text{parallel path, speed}\times\tfrac13$$
  17. speed × $\frac13$ ⇒ KE × $\frac19$$$\text{KE lost}=1-\left(\tfrac13\right)^2=\tfrac89$$
M3
Oblique impact with a condition: moves ⊥ line of centresuses S3 F5

Smooth sphere $A$ (mass $m$) moves with speed $u$ on a smooth horizontal surface and hits smooth sphere $B$ (mass $km$), equal radius, at rest. Just before impact, $A$'s velocity makes angle $\theta$ with the line of centres. Coefficient of restitution $e$.

  1. Show that $B$'s speed after is $\dfrac{u(1+e)\cos\theta}{1+k}$.
  2. After the impact $A$ moves ⊥ the line of centres. Show that $ek=1$ and deduce that $k\ge1$.

Now $e=\frac12$, $k=2$, and the impact loses 40% of the KE.

  1. Find $\tan\theta$.
  2. Show that $A$ and $B$ move off at right angles with equal speeds.
  1. start: $B$ at rest$$m_A=m,\ \ m_B=km,\qquad u_A=u,\ \ u_B=0$$
  2. at impact: line of centres, angle $\theta$ from it$$\text{line of centres}=\text{through both centres}$$
  3. resolve $A$: along line of centres, across it$$\text{along }u\cos\theta,\qquad\text{across }u\sin\theta$$
  4. impulse only along line of centres ⇒ $A$ keeps $u\sin\theta$ across; $B$ gets no across part$$B\text{ moves along line of centres}$$
  5. (a) momentum along line of centres, masses in$$mu\cos\theta+km(0)=mx+kmy$$
  6. NEL along line of centres$$y-x=e\,(u\cos\theta-0)$$
  7. momentum ÷ $m$, add NEL: $x$ cancels$$y=\frac{u(1+e)\cos\theta}{1+k}$$
  8. $x=y-eu\cos\theta$$$x=\frac{u(1-ek)\cos\theta}{1+k}$$
  9. (b) $A$ ⊥ line of centres ⇔ its along part $x=0$$$1-ek=0\iff ek=1$$
  10. $e=\frac1k$ and $0\le e\le1$$$\tfrac1k\le1\iff k\ge1$$
  11. now $e=\frac12$, $k=2$ ($ek=1$ ✓)$$x=0,\qquad y=\frac{u\left(1+\frac12\right)\cos\theta}{3}=\tfrac12u\cos\theta$$
  12. (c) KE lost, masses in: only the along part changed$$\tfrac12mu^2-\Big[\tfrac12m(u\sin\theta)^2+\tfrac12(2m)\left(\tfrac12u\cos\theta\right)^2\Big]=\tfrac12mu^2\cdot\tfrac12\cos^2\theta$$
  13. lost = 40% of $\frac12mu^2$$$\tfrac12\cos^2\theta=\tfrac25\iff\cos^2\theta=\tfrac45\iff\tan\theta=\tfrac12$$
  14. (d) $\sin\theta=\frac1{\sqrt5}$, $\cos\theta=\frac2{\sqrt5}$$$A:\ u\sin\theta=\tfrac{u}{\sqrt5},\qquad B:\ \tfrac12u\cos\theta=\tfrac{u}{\sqrt5}$$
  15. $A$ across, $B$ along the line of centres ⇒ at right angles, equal speeds$$A\perp B,\qquad |v_A|=|v_B|=\tfrac{u}{\sqrt5}$$
