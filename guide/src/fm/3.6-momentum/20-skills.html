Skills

S1
Direct impact of two spheresuses F1

When: spheres moving along the line of their centres collide directly: "find, in terms of $u$ and $e$, the velocities of $A$ and $B$ after the collision".

  1. Sketch before/after with masses. Pick + direction ($A$ → $B$). Draw both unknown velocities after in the + direction.
  2. Momentum: $m_Au_A+m_Bu_B=m_Av_A+m_Bv_B$, signed, masses in.
  3. NEL: $v_B-v_A=e(u_A-u_B)$, $A$ on the − side.
  4. Solve the pair. Negative answer ⇒ moves in − direction.
"Direction of $A$ is reversed" / "$A$ is brought to rest"
  • $v_A\lt 0$ / $v_A=0$, + = $A$'s old direction. Solve for $e$; intersect with $0\le e\le1$.
Condition gives a quadratic in $e$
  • Keep every root in $0\le e\le1$, $e=1$ included. Often both are valid. Mass ratio $k$: reject $k\le0$.
S2
Chains: wall, then further collisionsuses F1 F2

When: 3 spheres in a line, or $B$ then hits a wall square on: "show that there is a second collision between $A$ and $B$", "show that there are no further collisions", "find the set of values of $e$ for which…".

  1. One + direction for the whole question. List every velocity after each impact.
  2. Wall square on: velocity × $(-e_{\text{wall}})$ (F2).
  3. Collision between $X$ (behind, − side) and $Y$ (ahead) ⇔ $v_X\gt v_Y$.
  4. It happens ⇒ S1 again with the new velocities.
  5. No further collisions ⇔ behind ≤ ahead for every neighbouring pair, nobody heading for a wall.
"Find the set of values of $e$" for (no) further collision
  • Velocities in $e$ ⇒ inequality $v_X\gt v_Y$ (or $\le$). Solve, intersect with $0\le e\le1$. Same $e$ in every impact unless told otherwise.
S3
Oblique impact of two spheresuses F3 F5

When: velocities at angles to the line of centres: "$A$ moves with speed $u$ at angle $\alpha$ to the line of centres…", "find the speed and direction of each sphere after", "angle through which $A$ is deflected".

  1. Line of centres at impact (through both centres). + along it from $A$ to $B$.
  2. Resolve every velocity: along ($u\cos\alpha$), across ($u\sin\alpha$).
  3. Across: each sphere keeps its own across part.
  4. Along: S1 with the along parts only. Masses in. Signs in NEL match momentum.
  5. Recombine each sphere: speed $=\sqrt{\text{along}^2+\text{across}^2}$, angle with line of centres $=\tan^{-1}\dfrac{\text{across}}{\text{along}}$.
$B$ at rest
  • $B$ has no across part ⇒ $B$ moves off along the line of centres.
$B$'s direction given against $A$'s path (e.g. "$B$ moves ⊥ $A$'s path")
  • Angle of $B$ with line of centres $=90^\circ-\theta$ ⇒ along $=u_B\sin\theta$, across $=u_B\cos\theta$.
  • Coming towards $A$ along the line ⇒ along part negative (+ is $A$ → $B$). Approach $=u_A\cos\theta+u_B\sin\theta$.
"After the collision, $A$ (or $B$) moves ⊥ the line of centres"
  • That sphere's along part after $=0$. Put 0 into momentum and NEL.
"$A$ and $B$ move in the same / parallel directions after"
  • Same direction ⇔ $\dfrac{\text{across}}{\text{along}}$ equal for both. Final ∥ some initial direction ⇔ same ratio as that one.
Deflection angle; along part after is negative
  • Measure each velocity's angle from the + line of centres, 0°–180°. Along part negative ⇒ $180^\circ-\tan^{-1}\left|\dfrac{\text{across}}{\text{along}}\right|$.
  • Deflection = new angle − old angle (across part keeps its side, so both are on the same side).
KE condition: "loses 70% of KE", "KE of $A$ = KE of $B$"
  • Full speeds: $\tfrac12m(\text{along}^2+\text{across}^2)$, masses in. Across parts are the same both sides ⇒ cancel.
  • Sub along parts in terms of $k$ (or $e$); divide by $u^2$; given $\tan\theta$ ⇒ $\cos^2\theta$ from a right triangle ⇒ equation in $k$ alone.
"Find the range of values of $k$"
  • Make $e$ the subject in terms of $k$, then use $0\le e\le1$.
S4
Oblique impact with a wall or planeuses F4

When: "a particle strikes a fixed smooth wall / barrier / plane at angle $\alpha$ … rebounds at angle $\beta$": find $e$, the speed after, the angle, the KE lost.

  1. Resolve: along surface $u\cos\alpha$, into surface $u\sin\alpha$ ($\alpha$ with the surface).
  2. Along kept. Into × $e$, reversed. Use speeds, no minus signs.
  3. $\tan\beta=e\tan\alpha$; speed $=\sqrt{\text{along}^2+\text{out}^2}$.
Angle given with the normal, not the surface
  • Swap: along $=u\sin\theta$, into $=u\cos\theta$.
Two walls at right angles
  • Along part of wall 1 = into part of wall 2. Angle with wall 2 after = $90^\circ-\alpha$ ($\alpha$ = first angle with wall 1).
  • After both: velocity = $-e\,\times$ velocity before ⇒ path ∥ original, opposite sense; speed × $e$, KE × $e^2$.
Two walls at another angle, e.g. 60°
  • Triangle: wall 1, wall 2, path between them. (angle leaving wall 1) + (angle between walls) + (angle meeting wall 2) = 180°. Then F4 again.
"Direction turned through 90°"
  • Turn $=180^\circ-\alpha-\beta$. So 90° ⇔ $\alpha+\beta=90^\circ$ ⇒ $\tan\beta=\cot\alpha$ ⇒ $e\tan^2\alpha=1$.
Projectile hits a plane
  • Horizontal plane: horizontal part kept, vertical × $e$. Inclined plane: resolve along and ⊥ the plane, not horizontal/vertical.
