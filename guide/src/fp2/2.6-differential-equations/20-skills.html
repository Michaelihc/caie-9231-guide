Skills

S1
Separate variables

When: Question has $\frac{dy}{dx}=$ (only-$x$ stuff) × (only-$y$ stuff), e.g. $\frac{dy}{dx}=xe^{-y}$. Or a substitution turned it into that.

  1. Get all $y$ with $dy$ on left, all $x$ with $dx$ on right.
  2. Integrate both sides. One $+C$, on the right.
  3. Initial condition ⇒ $C$. Rearrange for $y$ if asked.
  1. start$$\frac{dy}{dx}=2x\,e^{-y}$$
  2. all $y$ with $dy$, all $x$ with $dx$$$e^{y}\,dy=2x\,dx$$
  3. integral sign on both sides$$\int e^{y}\,dy=\int 2x\,dx$$
  4. integrate, one $+C$$$e^{y}=x^{2}+C$$
  5. make $y$ the subject$$y=\ln\left(x^{2}+C\right)$$
S2
1st order linear: integrating factor

When: 1st order: only $\frac{dy}{dx}$, no $\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}$. $y$ and $\frac{dy}{dx}$ appear on their own, not squared, not multiplied together. Shape: (something)$\frac{dy}{dx}$ + (something)$\,y$ = (something), e.g. $x\frac{dy}{dx}+2y=e^x$.

  1. Divide every term by coefficient of $\frac{dy}{dx}$ ⇒ $\frac{dy}{dx}+P\,y=Q$.
  2. IF $=e^{\int P\,dx}$. Simplify. No $+C$.
  3. Work out IF × $Q$ (simplify). Write $\frac{d}{dx}(\text{IF}\cdot y)=\text{IF}\cdot Q$.
  4. Integrate right side ⇒ IF × $y$ = that $+\,C$. Add $+C$ now.
  5. Initial condition ⇒ $C$.
  6. Divide whole thing by IF. Answer must start $y=\dots$
  1. start: $y=5$ when $x=2$$$x\frac{dy}{dx}+2y=4x^{2}$$
  2. divide every term by $x$$$\frac{dy}{dx}+\frac{2}{x}\,y=4x$$
  3. IF $=e^{\int P\,dx}$$$\text{IF}=e^{\int\frac{2}{x}dx}=e^{2\ln x}=x^{2}$$
  4. multiply every term by IF$$x^{2}\frac{dy}{dx}+2x\,y=4x^{3}$$
  5. left side is one derivative$$\frac{d}{dx}\left(x^{2}y\right)=4x^{3}$$
  6. integral sign on both sides$$x^{2}y=\int 4x^{3}\,dx$$
  7. integrate, $+C$ now$$x^{2}y=x^{4}+C$$
  8. initial condition: $x=2$, $y=5$$$4\times5=16+C\ \Rightarrow\ C=4$$
  9. divide by IF$$y=x^{2}+\frac{4}{x^{2}}$$
Simplifying $e^{\int P}$
  • Hyperbolic: $\tanh x\to\cosh x$, $2\tanh x\to\cosh^2x$, $\coth x\to\sinh x$.
  • $\frac1{\sqrt{x^2+1}}\to e^{\sinh^{-1}x}=x+\sqrt{x^2+1}$ (log form of $\sinh^{-1}$).
Right side hard to integrate
  • $e^{ax}\sin bx$: by parts twice, same choice of $u$ ⇒ same integral comes back ⇒ move to left, divide.
  • Root of quadratic: MF19 $\sinh^{-1}$/$\cosh^{-1}$ rows.
S3
2nd order constant coefficient: CF + PI

When: 2nd order with plain-number coefficients: $a\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}+b\frac{dy}{dx}+cy=$ (function of $x$), e.g. $\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}+4\frac{dy}{dx}+5y=10\sin x$.

  1. Auxiliary equation ⇒ roots ⇒ CF.
  2. Trial PI from right side. Check it is not in CF.
  3. Differentiate trial twice. Sub trial and its derivatives into left side.
  4. Compare coefficients ⇒ unknowns.
  5. Write $y=$ CF + PI (in question's variable: $x$ or $t$).
  1. start$$\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}+4\frac{dy}{dx}+13y=80\sin x$$
  2. auxiliary equation$$m^{2}+4m+13=0$$
  3. roots$$m=-2\pm3i$$
  4. complex roots $\Rightarrow$ CF$$y=e^{-2x}(A\cos3x+B\sin3x)$$
  5. trial PI: shape of right side ($\sin x$), both cos and sin$$\mathllap{y={}}\lambda\cos x+\mu\sin x$$
  6. differentiate$\dfrac{d}{dx}$$$\mathllap{\frac{dy}{dx}={}}{-}\lambda\sin x+\mu\cos x$$
  7. differentiate again$\dfrac{d}{dx}$$$\mathllap{\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}={}}{-}\lambda\cos x-\mu\sin x$$
  8. copy the equation down, one term per row$$\begin{aligned}&\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}\\[3pt]&\mathllap{{}+4}\frac{dy}{dx}\\[3pt]&\mathllap{{}+13}y\\[3pt]&\mathllap{{}={}}80\sin x\end{aligned}$$
  9. sub in $\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}$$$\begin{aligned}&\mathllap{(}{-}\lambda\cos x-\mu\sin x)\\[3pt]&\mathllap{{}+4}\frac{dy}{dx}\\[3pt]&\mathllap{{}+13}y\\[3pt]&\mathllap{{}={}}80\sin x\end{aligned}$$
  10. sub in $\frac{dy}{dx}$$$\begin{aligned}&\mathllap{(}{-}\lambda\cos x-\mu\sin x)\\[3pt]&\mathllap{{}+4(}{-}\lambda\sin x+\mu\cos x)\\[3pt]&\mathllap{{}+13}y\\[3pt]&\mathllap{{}={}}80\sin x\end{aligned}$$
  11. sub in $y$$$\begin{aligned}&\mathllap{(}{-}\lambda\cos x-\mu\sin x)\\[3pt]&\mathllap{{}+4(}{-}\lambda\sin x+\mu\cos x)\\[3pt]&\mathllap{{}+13(}\lambda\cos x+\mu\sin x)\\[3pt]&\mathllap{{}={}}80\sin x\end{aligned}$$
  12. collect cos terms, collect sin terms$$(12\lambda+4\mu)\cos x+(12\mu-4\lambda)\sin x=80\sin x$$
  13. compare coefficients$$12\lambda+4\mu=0,\ \ 12\mu-4\lambda=80\ \Rightarrow\ \lambda=-2,\ \mu=6$$
  14. $y=$ CF $+$ PI$$y=e^{-2x}(A\cos3x+B\sin3x)-2\cos x+6\sin x$$
Right side only $\sin$ or only $\cos$

Trial needs both: $\lambda\cos px+\mu\sin px$.

Right side $27x^2$ only

Trial needs full $\lambda x^2+\mu x+\nu$. Compare $x^2$, $x$, constant terms.

Trial in CF: form given, e.g. $kxe^{-x}$ or $kx^2e^{2x}$

Differentiate given form with product rule. Sub. Solve for $k$. ($x^2$ when root is repeated.)

S4
Particular solution from initial conditions

When: Question gives values, e.g. "$y=1$ and $\frac{dy}{dx}=0$ when $x=0$", and asks for "the particular solution" or "the solution for which …".

  1. Start from full general solution (CF + PI). Keep both $A$, $B$.
  2. Sub $x=0$, $y=$ given ⇒ equation 1.
  3. Differentiate general solution. Sub $x=0$, $\frac{dy}{dx}=$ given ⇒ equation 2.
  4. Solve simultaneously. Write $y=\dots$ with numbers.
  1. general solution; $y=0$, $\frac{dy}{dx}=0$ when $x=0$$$y=e^{-2x}(A\cos3x+B\sin3x)-2\cos x+6\sin x$$
  2. sub $x=0$, $y=0$$$0=A-2\ \Rightarrow\ A=2$$
  3. differentiate (product rule)$$\frac{dy}{dx}=e^{-2x}\big((3B-2A)\cos3x-(3A+2B)\sin3x\big)+2\sin x+6\cos x$$
  4. sub $x=0$, $\frac{dy}{dx}=0$$$0=3B-2A+6\ \Rightarrow\ B=-\tfrac23$$
  5. particular solution$$y=e^{-2x}\left(2\cos3x-\tfrac23\sin3x\right)-2\cos x+6\sin x$$
After a substitution

Convert conditions to new variable first. E.g. $v=y^4$: $v(0)=y(0)^4$, $v'=4y^3y'$. $y=\cosh u$: $y'=\sinh u\cdot u'$.

S5
Given substitution ⇒ "show that equation becomes …"

When: Question hands you a substitution ($v=y^4$, $x=e^t$, $y=ux$, …) and says "show that the equation becomes …".

  1. Write substitution. Differentiate it with respect to $x$: chain rule on $y$ terms, product rule on products.
  2. 2nd order target ⇒ differentiate again.
  3. Start from the shown equation's left side. Replace new variable and its derivatives by their old-variable forms. Simplify ⇒ it becomes (a multiple of) the original equation's left side ⇒ swap in original right side ⇒ matches.
  4. Solve new equation (S1, S2 or S3).
  5. Sub back ⇒ answer in original variables, e.g. $v=y^4$ ⇒ $y=v^{\frac14}$, $x=e^t$ ⇒ $t=\ln x$.
  1. start$$x^{2}\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}-4x\frac{dy}{dx}+6y=0$$
  2. swap: $x\frac{dy}{dx}\to\frac{dy}{dt}$, $x^2\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}\to\frac{d^2y}{dt^2}-\frac{dy}{dt}$$$\left(\frac{d^2y}{dt^2}-\frac{dy}{dt}\right)-4\frac{dy}{dt}+6y=0$$
  3. simplify: constant coefficients$$\frac{d^2y}{dt^2}-5\frac{dy}{dt}+6y=0$$
  4. auxiliary equation$$m^{2}-5m+6=0\ \Rightarrow\ m=2,\ 3$$
  5. CF in $t$$$y=Ae^{2t}+Be^{3t}$$
  6. back: $e^{t}=x$$$y=Ax^{2}+Bx^{3}$$
$x=e^t$

Use $x\frac{dy}{dx}=\frac{dy}{dt}$, $x^2\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}=\frac{d^2y}{dt^2}-\frac{dy}{dt}$. Back: $t=\ln x$.

$\frac{dy}{dx}=f\!\left(\frac yx\right)$ with $y=ux$

$\frac{dy}{dx}=u+x\frac{du}{dx}$ ⇒ $x\frac{du}{dx}=f(u)-u$ ⇒ separable (S1).

S6
Behaviour for large $x$

When: Question says "for large $x$", "as $x\to\infty$", "approximate solution", or "what happens to $y$".

  1. CF terms with $e^{-kx}$ ($k>0$) → 0 as $x\to\infty$. Say so.
  2. Approximate solution for large $x$: $y\approx$ PI.
  1. particular solution$$y=e^{-2x}\left(2\cos3x-\tfrac23\sin3x\right)-2\cos x+6\sin x$$
  2. $e^{-2x}\to0$ as $x\to\infty$$$y\approx6\sin x-2\cos x$$
  3. R-formula$$R=\sqrt{6^{2}+2^{2}}=2\sqrt{10},\quad \tan\varphi=-\tfrac26$$
  4. large-$x$ answer$$y\approx2\sqrt{10}\,\sin(x-0.322)$$
