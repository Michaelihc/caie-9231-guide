Formulas

IF
Integrating factor
$$\frac{dy}{dx}+P(x)\,y=Q(x)\;\Rightarrow\;\text{IF}=e^{\int P\,dx},\qquad \frac{d}{dx}\big(\text{IF}\cdot y\big)=\text{IF}\cdot Q$$

Coefficient of $\frac{dy}{dx}$ must be 1 first. No $+C$ in IF.

Make dy/dx stand alone. IF = $e$ to the integral of what multiplies $y$.

Times everything by IF ⇒ left side = derivative of (IF × $y$).

  1. Want IF × left side to be one product-rule derivative: (IF × $y$)′ = IF × $y'$ + IF′ × $y$.
  2. IF × left side = IF × $y'$ + IF × ($y$'s coefficient) × $y$. Match ⇒ IF′ = ($y$'s coefficient) × IF.
  3. Separate, integrate: ln IF = ∫ ($y$'s coefficient) d$x$ ⇒ IF = $e^{\int y\text{'s coefficient}\,dx}$.
F1
General solution = CF + PI
$$y=\underbrace{y_{\text{CF}}}_{\text{RHS}=0,\ \text{has constants}}+\underbrace{y_{\text{PI}}}_{\text{any one solution, no constants}}$$

Complementary function (CF): solves equation with right side = 0. Holds all arbitrary constants.

Particular integral (PI): one solution of full equation. No constants.

Learn it: definitions, nothing to derive.

Picture: change initial conditions ⇒ start changes, end always settles onto PI.

F2
Auxiliary equation + CF
$$a\,y''+b\,y'+c\,y=0\;\Rightarrow\;am^2+bm+c=0$$
$$\begin{array}{ll}\text{2 real roots }\alpha\ne\beta: & y=Ae^{\alpha x}+Be^{\beta x}\\ \text{repeated root }\alpha: & y=(A+Bx)e^{\alpha x}\\ \text{complex }p\pm qi: & y=e^{px}(A\cos qx+B\sin qx)\end{array}$$

1st order $a\,y'+b\,y=0$: $m=-\frac ba$, $y=Ae^{mx}$.

Swap $y''\to m^2$, $y'\to m$, $y\to1$. Solve for $m$.

2 different real roots ⇒ 2 exponentials. Same root twice ⇒ $(A+Bx)$ × exponential. Complex ⇒ real part goes in $e^{px}$, imaginary part is the cos/sin frequency.

Picture: complex roots ⇒ imaginary part = wiggle speed, real part = envelope (below 0 dies, above 0 grows).

  1. Try $y=e^{mx}$: $y'=me^{mx}$, $y''=m^2e^{mx}$.
  2. Sub in, divide by $e^{mx}$ (never 0) ⇒ $am^2+bm+c=0$.
  3. Complex $p\pm qi$: $e^{(p\pm qi)x}=e^{px}(\cos qx\pm i\sin qx)$ ⇒ combine into $e^{px}(A\cos qx+B\sin qx)$.

Repeated root: only one exponential, so second solution is $x\times$ it. Learn this case.

F3
PI trial form
$$\begin{array}{ll}\text{RHS} & \text{try}\\ \hline \text{polynomial, top power }x^n & \lambda_nx^n+\dots+\lambda_1x+\lambda_0\\ ae^{kx} & \lambda e^{kx}\\ a\cos px\text{ and/or }b\sin px & \lambda\cos px+\mu\sin px\end{array}$$

Sum on RHS ⇒ sum of trials. Trial already in CF ⇒ ×$x$ (×$x^2$ if repeated root). Papers give this form.

Copy the shape of the right side with unknown numbers.

Polynomial: every power up to the top one. Only sin or only cos: still use both.

  1. Trial must survive differentiating: derivatives of $x^n$ give every lower power ⇒ need them all.
  2. $(\cos px)'=-p\sin px$, $(\sin px)'=p\cos px$ ⇒ each turns into the other ⇒ need both.
  3. Trial already in CF ⇒ it gives 0 on left side ⇒ useless ⇒ times $x$.
F4
Substitution $x=e^t$
$$x\frac{dy}{dx}=\frac{dy}{dt},\qquad x^2\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}=\frac{d^2y}{dt^2}-\frac{dy}{dt}$$

Turns $ax^2y''+bxy'+cy$ into constant coefficients. Back: $t=\ln x$.

$x$ times first derivative = first $t$-derivative.

$x^2$ times second derivative = second $t$-derivative minus first $t$-derivative.

  1. Chain rule: $\frac{dy}{dt}=\frac{dy}{dx}\cdot\frac{dx}{dt}=\frac{dy}{dx}\cdot e^t=x\frac{dy}{dx}$.
  2. Differentiate again in $t$, product rule: $\frac{d^2y}{dt^2}=x\frac{dy}{dx}+x\cdot\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}\cdot x$.
  3. Rearrange: $x^2\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}=\frac{d^2y}{dt^2}-\frac{dy}{dt}$.
F5
Substitution $y=ux$
$$y=ux\;\Rightarrow\;\frac{dy}{dx}=u+x\frac{du}{dx}$$

For $\frac{dy}{dx}=f\!\left(\frac yx\right)$. Gives separable equation in $u,x$.

dy/dx = $u$ + $x$ times du/dx.

Product rule on $u\cdot x$: $u'\cdot x+u\cdot1$.

F6
Amplitude form
$$\lambda\sin x+\mu\cos x=R\sin(x+\varphi),\qquad R=\sqrt{\lambda^2+\mu^2},\quad\tan\varphi=\frac{\mu}{\lambda}$$

From A Level Maths (9709). Used for large-$x$ answers (S6).

Amplitude = square root of (sin coefficient² + cos coefficient²).

  1. Expand: $R\sin(x+\varphi)=R\cos\varphi\,\sin x+R\sin\varphi\,\cos x$.
  2. Match: sin coefficient $=R\cos\varphi$, cos coefficient $=R\sin\varphi$.
  3. Square and add ⇒ $R$. Divide ⇒ $\tan\varphi$.
d/dx
Derivatives you needMF19 + ★ rows
$$\begin{array}{lll} \text{function} & \text{derivative} & \\ \hline x^n & nx^{n-1} & \text{MF19}\\ e^{kx} & ke^{kx} & \bigstar\\ \sin kx & k\cos kx & \bigstar\\ \cos kx & -k\sin kx & \bigstar\\ \ln x & \frac1x & \text{MF19}\\ u\cdot v & u'v+uv' & \text{MF19}\\ y^n,\ y\text{ depends on }x & ny^{n-1}\frac{dy}{dx} & \bigstar \end{array}$$

★ = chain rule: whatever is inside, its derivative multiplies on. Same rule: derivative of $\left(\frac{dy}{dx}\right)^2$ is $2\frac{dy}{dx}\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}$.

Worked shape: $\left(e^{px}\cos qx\right)'=pe^{px}\cos qx-qe^{px}\sin qx$ (product rule + ★ rows).

Number in front of $x$ comes out to the front. Something that depends on $x$ inside ⇒ times its own derivative.

Learn the ★ rows. The rest is in MF19.

∫
Integrals you needMF19 + ★ rows
$$\begin{array}{lll} \text{function} & \text{integral} & \\ \hline x^n & \frac{x^{n+1}}{n+1}\ (n\ne-1) & \text{MF19}\\ \frac1x & \ln x & \text{MF19}\\ e^{kx} & \frac1k e^{kx} & \bigstar\\ \sin kx & -\frac1k\cos kx & \bigstar\\ \cos kx & \frac1k\sin kx & \bigstar\\ \frac{f'(x)}{f(x)} & \ln f(x) & \text{MF19}\\ u\,v' & uv-\int u'v\,dx\ \ (\text{by parts}) & \text{MF19} \end{array}$$

By parts: $u$ = the part that gets simpler when differentiated (the $x$ or $x^2$). $e^{ax}\sin bx$ or $e^{ax}\cos bx$: by parts twice ⇒ same integral comes back ⇒ move it to left side, divide.

★ rows: divide by the number in front of $x$. Add $+C$ once, at the end.

Learn the ★ rows. The rest is in MF19.
